          How GO GREEN Creates Environmental Awareness.

          1. Discover environmental concerns.
          2. Explore NASA FIRMS hotspot data.
          3. Ask EcoBot for explanations.
          4. Create a demo environmental report.
          5. Complete a green action challenge.
          6. Calculate estimated environmental contributions.

          Why GO GREEN?

          Interactive maps, education, local reporting demos, and gamified actions can help connect knowledge with practical action. This prototype makes no claims of proven real-world outcomes.
